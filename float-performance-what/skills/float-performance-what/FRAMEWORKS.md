# Float's 5 Business Impact Areas

Your work should map to one or more of these areas when documenting performance review impact.

## 1. Customer Acquisition

- Won new customers or generated pipeline (Product or GTM)
- Launched features/experiments that improved conversion
- Shipped partner/integration programs that drove leads
- Improved targeting/segmentation or lifecycle journeys
- Provided enablement that supported customer acquisition
- Built enablement that helped close deals faster
- Discovered key learnings or playbooks that enabled the above

## 2. Customer Satisfaction

- Increased NPS or reduced churn
- Reduced support tickets, bugs, or repeat customer pain
- Improved onboarding, activation, or time-to-value
- Enabled trusted/compliant customer experiences
- Saved customers at risk of churn
- Built playbooks or programs that scaled our retention capabilities
- Discovered key learnings or playbooks that enabled the above

## 3. Revenue Growth

- Launched monetizable products/features/playbooks that increased TPV, FX, Yield volume, SaaS, and/or ARPU
- Crafted commercial launches that led to revenue growth
- Drove expansion and monetization wins through upsell, cross-sell, renewals, and improved pricing/packaging
- Unlocked pricing, packaging, or monetization wins
- Created programs/assets that directly drove adoption or expansion
- Discovered key learnings or playbooks that enabled the above

## 4. Product Innovation

- Shipped customer-facing product capabilities (new workflows, features, integrations) that advanced the roadmap
- Improved product usability/accessibility or reduced feature friction
- Strengthened product discovery (research, instrumentation, insights) that changed product direction or priorities
- Improved experiment capability (A/B, analytics, feature flags) that increased learning velocity
- Discovered key learnings or playbooks that enabled the above

## 5. Operational Excellence

- Increased engineering velocity (dev experience, testing, tooling)
- Improved reliability and stability (uptime, incidents, on-call health)
- Built platform foundations that sped up product delivery
- Simplified/automated workflows that saved time or headcount
- Reduced internal friction, decision thrash, or cross-team handoffs
- Reduced risk and losses (fraud, security, compliance, audit)
- Reduced infra/vendor/tooling spend while maintaining outcomes
- Discovered key learnings or playbooks that enabled the above

---

## How to Use

When documenting your impact:
1. For each outcome, identify which impact area(s) apply
2. Provide specific evidence from that area's indicators
3. Quantify the impact where possible
4. Many outcomes touch multiple areas - that's great! Call them all out with specific evidence.
