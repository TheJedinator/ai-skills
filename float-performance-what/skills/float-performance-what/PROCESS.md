## The Process

### Phase 1: Brainstorm Your Work

**Goal:** Generate a comprehensive list of everything you worked on during the review period.

**Prompts to help you remember:**
- What projects did you lead or contribute to?
- What problems did you solve?
- What features or capabilities did you ship?
- What systems or processes did you improve?
- What cross-functional initiatives were you part of?
- What customer-facing work did you do?
- What technical decisions did you make or influence?
- What did you build, fix, or optimize?
- What research or discovery work did you do?
- What experiments did you run?
- What incidents or critical issues did you handle?

**Output:** A raw, unfiltered list of activities and projects (don't self-censor yet)

---

### Phase 2: Connect Activities to Outcomes

**Goal:** For each activity, identify the concrete outcome or result.

**Critical Distinction:** 
- **Activity:** "I built a new API endpoint"
- **Outcome:** "The new API endpoint enabled partner integrations that drove 50 new leads"

**For each activity, ask:**
- What changed as a result of this work?
- Who benefited? (Customers, team, company, partners?)
- What metrics improved? (Be specific with numbers)
- What problem no longer exists because of this work?
- What became possible that wasn't before?
- What would have happened if you hadn't done this work?

**Red Flags (outcomes you need to strengthen):**
- "Improved efficiency" (by how much? for whom?)
- "Enhanced quality" (what metric? what was the impact?)
- "Helped the team" (how? what specific result?)
- "Contributed to X" (what specifically was your contribution?)

**Output:** Each activity paired with its concrete outcome

---

### Phase 3: Map to Business Impact Areas

**Goal:** Connect your outcomes to Float's 5 business impact areas.

**For each outcome, identify:**
- Which business impact area(s) does this affect?
- What specific indicators from that area apply?
- Can you quantify the business impact?

**Examples of Good Mapping:**

**Weak:**
- "Built feature X" → Product Innovation

**Strong:**
- "Shipped feature X that reduced customer onboarding time by 30% (from 10 days to 7 days), improving activation rates by 15%" → Customer Satisfaction + Product Innovation

**Weak:**
- "Improved API performance" → Operational Excellence

**Strong:**
- "Optimized API performance reducing p95 latency by 200ms, which decreased page load times by 40% and reduced support tickets about slow dashboard by 60%" → Customer Satisfaction + Operational Excellence

**Multiple Impact Areas:**
Many outcomes touch multiple areas - that's great! Call them all out with specific evidence.

**Output:** Activities mapped to impact areas with clear business outcomes

---

### Phase 4: Quantify and Validate

**Goal:** Ensure every outcome is specific, measurable, and accurately represents your contribution.

**Checklist for each outcome:**
- ☐ **Specific:** Exact numbers, percentages, or clear before/after states
- ☐ **Measurable:** Quantitative metrics where possible (time saved, costs reduced, revenue increased, conversion improved, errors reduced)
- ☐ **Your Contribution:** Clear about YOUR role vs. team's role
- ☐ **Business Impact:** Explicitly connected to Float's impact areas
- ☐ **Outcome not Activity:** Describes the result, not just the work

**Questions to strengthen weak outcomes:**
- **Vague metrics:** What's the specific number? What was the baseline?
- **Unclear contribution:** What specifically did YOU do vs. what did the team do?
- **Missing context:** What was the scale? How many customers/users affected?
- **Activity-focused:** What was the result of doing that activity?

**Output:** A validated, quantified list of outcomes mapped to business impact areas

---

### Phase 5: Assess Scope and Significance

**Goal:** Understand the breadth and depth of your impact using the leveling framework lens.

**Scope Indicators:**
- **Team-level:** Primarily affected your immediate team
- **Cross-functional:** Extended to partner teams or workflows  
- **Org-level:** Influenced broader priorities or multiple teams
- **Company-level:** Materially shaped business outcomes or strategy

**For each outcome, consider:**
- How many people/teams were affected?
- How many customers were impacted?
- How long will this impact last? (One-time fix vs. ongoing system)
- What level of ambiguity/complexity did you navigate?
- Did you define the problem, or was it given to you?
- Did you scope the solution, or follow a plan?

**Output:** Outcomes ranked by scope and significance
